Reading notes: Thinking, Fast and Slow

System 1 operates automatically and quickly, with little or no effort and no sense of voluntary control. System 2 allocates attention to the effortful mental activities that demand it.

The availability heuristic is a mental shortcut that relies on immediate examples that come to mind. Losses loom larger than gains. People work harder to avoid losing what they have than to acquire something of equal value.
